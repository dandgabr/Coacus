# Esports Integrity Governance

This skill guides the AI to govern competitive integrity, where the threat includes fraud and corruption, not only cheating.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Integrity Framework

- A published **rulebook** and **code of conduct** with defined offences, evidence standards and sanctions.
- An **integrity program** covering player, team, coach and official conduct.
- A **sanction register** and interim-suspension mechanism so enforcement is consistent and visible.
- An independent **tribunal** for adjudication and dispute resolution, with an appeals path.

Bodies such as the Esports Integrity Commission operate these mechanisms and collaborate with wider sport and law-enforcement bodies.

---

## 🎯 2. Threats

| Threat | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Match-fixing** | Deliberately losing or manipulating outcomes for betting profit |
| **Betting fraud** | Insider information, spot-fixing, betting syndicates |
| **Cheating** | The technical integrity threat (see the anti-cheat skill) |
| **Doping / substance abuse** | Performance-enhancing or cognitive stimulants |
| **Harassment and misconduct** | Safety and reputation risk |
| **Spectator-bug exploitation** | Using a broadcast feature to gain competitive advantage |

---

## 🛡️ 3. Detection and Enforcement

- Monitor betting markets for anomalies correlated with match events.
- Educate players on prohibited conduct and reporting channels.
- Protect whistleblowers; most integrity cases surface through a report.
- Apply proportionate, documented sanctions and publish outcomes where appropriate.
- Coordinate with regulators, leagues and law enforcement for criminal conduct.
