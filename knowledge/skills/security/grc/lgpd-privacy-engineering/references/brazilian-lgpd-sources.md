# Brazilian LGPD sources and status

Reviewed 2026-11-27. Recheck the consolidated law, the Official Gazette (DOU)
and the [ANPD regulations directory](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/)
before applying a provision to a live project. Record the act, article,
effective date, applicability, link and date checked. Check any applicable
sector-specific rule. Treat an unreadable source or unresolved amendment as
unverified.

## Interpretation and guidance

- [ANPD Interpretive Statement 1/2023](https://bibliotecadigital.mj.gov.br/bitstream/1/10215/2/Enunciado_ANPD_2023_1.html)
  interprets the use of Article 7 or 11 grounds for children and adolescents
  subject to the prevailing best interests of the child or adolescent. Apply
  Article 14 safeguards and the consent requirements when that basis applies.
- [ANPD legitimate interest guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia_legitimo_interesse.pdf/@@download/file)
  recommends a documented assessment of purpose, necessity, and balancing
  with safeguards. Article 10(3) allows the ANPD to request a RIPD when
  legitimate interest is used.
- [ANPD RIPD guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/agente-de-tratamento/relatorio-de-impacto-a-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-ripd)
  distinguishes a request by the Agency, risk-based preparation and prevention.
  Check Articles 5(XVII), 10(3) and 38 of the LGPD for definitions, possible
  requests and minimum content. Use the Resolution 2/2022 Article 4 test
  within its scope and as a risk input where appropriate. Record the controlling
  legal trigger or guidance separately.
- [ANPD simplified processing record](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-divulga-modelo-de-registro-simplificado-de-operacoes-com-dados-pessoais-para-agentes-de-tratamento-de-pequeno-porte-atpp),
  [role and officer guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia-orientativo-para-definicoes-dos-agentes-de-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-e-do-encarregado)
  and [cookie guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/processo-guia-orientativo-cookies-e-protecao-de-dados-pessoais.pdf)
  inform how to document facts. Apply the operative provisions and the product's
  actual behavior when making a decision.

## Procedural acts and open work

[ANPD Ordinance 16/2021](https://pesquisa.in.gov.br/imprensa/servlet/INPDFViewer?captchafield=firstAccess&data=09%2F07%2F2021&jornal=515&pagina=3)
governs the Agency's rulemaking process. The
[2026 revision consultation](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-abre-consulta-audiencia-publica-sobre-revisao-processo-regulatorio)
accepted comments until 23 November 2026. Use the
[regulatory agenda](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/regulacao/agenda-regulatoria-1)
to monitor proposals and consult the DOU for adopted instruments.
The tracking table updated 12 November 2026 listed the general RIPD and data
subject rights projects as in progress. A consultation, agenda or draft is
evidence of regulatory work. Base duties and deadlines on effective text.
