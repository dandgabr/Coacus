# UI Style: Silkpunk & Organic Engineering

Coined by author Ken Liu, silkpunk blends East Asian classical antiquity (Han and Tang dynasties) with speculative organic engineering. Features structures built of bamboo, silk, coconut fiber, paper lanterns, and steam or wind dynamics, articulated in sumi-e ink washes and origami geometry.

---

## 🧭 When to Activate

- Cultural literature platforms, East Asian fantasy and sci-fi games, historical archives, and expressive artistic sites.
- Synthesizing organic Asian traditional craftsmanship with futuristic mechanical interfaces.

---

## 🎨 Visual DNA

- **Palette:** Vermilion red (`#C3272B`), indigo blue (`#1D2A44`), bamboo stalk green (`#6A8D73`), aged mulberry paper (`#F5EFE0`), and hammered brass (`#C9A050`).
- **Type:** Calligraphic brush display fonts, elegant high-contrast serifs (Noto Serif CJK, Cinzel Decorative), paired with clean Asian grotesques.
- **Motifs:** Origami geometric fold seams, sumi-e ink wash gradients, cloud patterns (cloud scrolls), and bamboo-joint dividing bars.
- **Structure:** Vertical text reading tracks, pavilion lattice grids, and paper screen sliding panel animations.

---

## 🛠️ Implementation Notes

```css
.silkpunk-frame {
  background: #f5efe0;
  border: 2px solid #c9a050;
  box-shadow: inset 0 0 15px rgba(195, 39, 43, 0.08);

```

---

## ♿ Accessibility

- Brush calligraphy should be limited to titles and decorative elements; critical navigational copy requires legible glyph shapes.
- High contrast must be maintained between ink elements and background paper textures.
