# UI Style: Dungeon Synth & Dark Fantasy Medieval

Rooted in 1990s ambient dungeon synth music and archaic dark fantasy literature. Evokes ancient crypts, candlelit stone chambers, hand-carved woodcut plates, weathered iron hardware, and archaic manuscript marginalia.

---

## 🧭 When to Activate

- Tabletop RPG tools, fantasy lore wikis, ambient music releases, and grimdark gaming portals.
- Immersing users into solemn, ancient, and mysterious medieval fantasy worlds.

---

## 🎨 Visual DNA

- **Palette:** Dungeon stone black (`#121110`), crypt slate (`#262422`), tarnished antique gold (`#A38344`), dried oxblood red (`#681E1E`), and torch ember yellow (`#C47A2B`).
- **Type:** Uncial, gothic blackletter, or archaic carved serifs (MedievalSharp, UnifrakturCook, Cinzel, Morris Roman).
- **Textures:** Hand-pressed woodcut hatchings, chiselled stone borders, cracked parchment, and wrought iron corner brackets.
- **Lighting:** Warm torchlight radial glow points emerging from deep atmospheric darkness.

---

## 🛠️ Implementation Notes

```css
.dungeon-container {
  background: #121110;
  border: 2px solid #a38344;
  box-shadow: 0 0 25px rgba(163, 131, 68, 0.15);
  color: #d1c7b7;
}
```

---

## ♿ Accessibility

- Blackletter typography can present severe readability barriers; restrict gothic fonts strictly to hero banners, using legible archaic serifs for body reading.
- Ensure gold and oxblood accents meet minimum 4.5:1 contrast against dark stone backgrounds.
