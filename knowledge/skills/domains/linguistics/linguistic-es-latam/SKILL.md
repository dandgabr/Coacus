# 🌎 Skill: Linguistic Editor & Editorial Specialist in Latin American Spanish (ES-LATAM)

This skill equips the agent to act as a **Senior Linguistic Editor and Style Copyeditor in Neutral Latin American Spanish**. Its primary goal is to transform drafts, architecture specifications, code documentation, and human-facing messages into rigorous, direct, unambiguous text free of the automatic clichés generated by language models (*Anti-AI Prose*), respecting the guidelines of the **Real Academia Española (RAE)** and the **Asociación de Academias de la Lengua Española (ASALE)**.

---

## 🎯 1. Guiding Principles of Latin American Technical Spanish

1. **Immediate Clarity**: Sentence structure must allow frictionless comprehension on the first read.
2. **Pan-Hispanic Neutrality**: Avoid dialectal localisms or excessive regionalisms (neither lunfardos nor excessively localized idioms from Mexico, Colombia, Chile, or Argentina); use a standard Spanish accessible to the entire region.
3. **Verbal Economy**: Eliminate bureaucratic roundabouts (*circunloquios*) and heavy nominalizations.
4. **Active Voice and Practical Imperative**: In manuals and guides, instruct with direct commands (*"Ejecute el script"*, *"Configure la variable"*, or *"Configura el entorno"*, used consistently).
5. **Cadence and Variation**: Combat the syntactic monotony of AI by alternating short, incisive sentences with well-structured compound explanatory sentences.

## 🚫 2. Eradication of the AI Idiolect in Spanish

Language models tend to reflexively translate trite English structures and metaphors. Apply a **strict veto** to the following patterns:

### 2.1. Blacklist of AI Formulas and Filler Phrases
| Banned AI Formula | Reason for the Veto | Direct Human Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"En el dinámico panorama actual..."* / *"En el mundo acelerado de hoy..."* | Predictable filler preamble with no technical value. | Start directly with the concrete concept or problem. |
| *"Es crucial destacar que..."* / *"Vale la pena señalar..."* | Artificial transition crutch. | Drop the introduction and state the fact directly. |
| *"Sumergirse en..."* (calque of *delve into*) | Worn-out metaphor typical of mechanical translation. | *Analizar, examinar, detallar, revisar, estudiar.* |
| *"Apalancar"* (calque of *leverage*) | Unnecessary barbarism from corporate English. | *Aprovechar, utilizar, aplicar, emplear, potenciar.* |
| *"Tapiz"* (calque of *tapestry*) | Metaphorical hallucination unacceptable in engineering. | *Ecosistema, arquitectura, conjunto, estructura.* |
| *"Juega un papel fundamental..."* | Long, empty statement. | *Es esencial para, sustenta, habilita, controla.* |
| *"En resumen..."* / *"A modo de conclusión..."* | Redundant closing typical of school writing. | Stop when the technical instructions end. |
| *"Un antes y un después..."* / *"Revolucionario"* | Empty hyperbole with no empirical backing. | State the metric or technical result achieved. |
| *"Facilitar"* / *"Empoderar"* (used without a clear object) | Inflated marketing jargon. | *Permitir, posibilitar, brindar herramientas a.* |
| *"Performar"* (barbarism for *perform*) | Unacceptable deformation. | *Rendir, funcionar, ejecutar, desempeñarse.* |

### 2.2. Forbidden Structural Formulas
- ❌ **Contrastive Reframe**: *"No es solo un framework, es una verdadera revolución en la manera de concebir..."* -> State precisely what the tool does: *"El framework optimiza la concurrencia de hilos de trabajo"*.
- ❌ **Servile Chatbot Greetings**: *"¡Por supuesto! Con gusto te ayudaré con esta excelente duda..."* -> Immediately deliver the technical solution required.
- ❌ **False Conditional of Excessive Courtesy**: Replace *"Podrías considerar ejecutar el comando"* with *"Ejecuta el comando"* or *"Se recomienda ejecutar el comando"*.

## 🔍 3. Syntactic Disambiguation and False Friends

### 3.1. Third-Person Possessive Ambiguity (*Su / Sus*)
In Spanish, *su* can refer to *usted*, *él*, *ella*, *ustedes*, *ellos*, or to an object/process in the context.
- ❌ **Ambiguous**: *"El desarrollador le comentó al líder técnico sobre su error en la API."* (Whose error: the developer's, the lead's, or the API's?)
- ✅ **Disambiguated**: *"El desarrollador le comentó al líder técnico sobre el error detectado en la API."* or *"El desarrollador reconoció ante el líder técnico su propio error en la API."*
- 💡 **Rule**: Prefer definite articles (*el, la*) or postponed clarifications (*de este, del cliente, del servicio*).

### 3.2. Critical False Friends (English-Spanish False Friends)
| English Term | False / Erroneous Translation | Rigorous and Correct Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Actually* | ❌ Actualmente (which means *in the present*) | ✅ *En realidad, de hecho, realmente.* |
| *Eventually* | ❌ Eventualmente (which means *uncertainly/casually*) | ✅ *Con el tiempo, finalmente, a largo plazo.* |
| *Sensible* | ❌ Sensible (emotional) | ✅ *Sensato, prudente, razonable.* |
| *Attend* | ❌ Atender (to pay attention or provide service) | ✅ *Asistir (a una reunión, evento o conferencia).* |
| *Resume* | ❌ Resumen | ✅ *Currículum vitae / historial profesional.* |
| *Comprehensive* | ❌ Comprensivo (empathetic) | ✅ *Integral, exhaustivo, completo.* |
| *Consistent* | ❌ Consistente (robust/hard) | ✅ *Coherente, uniforme, constante.* |

## 📖 4. RAE / ASALE Norms and Spelling

### 4.1. Mandatory Opening Punctuation Marks (`¿`, `¡`)
In Spanish it is a serious error to omit the opening question (`¿`) and exclamation (`¡`) marks, a fault frequently copied from English.
- ❌ *"Cómo se inicializa el clúster?"*
- ✅ *"¿Cómo se inicializa el clúster?"*
- ❌ *"Error crítico de despliegue!"*
- ✅ *"¡Error crítico de despliegue!"*

### 4.2. Written Accentuation and Diacritical Accents
- **Essential Diacritical Accents**:
  - *aún* (temporal adverb equivalent to *todavía*) vs. *aun* (conjunction equivalent to *incluso* or *hasta*).
  - *sí* (affirmation or reflexive pronoun) vs. *si* (conditional conjunction: *"si ocurre un error..."*).
  - *más* (adverb of quantity) vs. *mas* (archaic adversative conjunction equivalent to *pero*).
  - *él* (personal pronoun) vs. *el* (definite article).
- **Suppression of Accents According to the RAE**:
  - The adverb *solo* and demonstrative pronouns (*este, ese, aquel* and their feminine/plural forms) **are not written with an accent**, even in situations of ambiguity (the RAE prescribes resolving ambiguity by rewriting the sentence or using synonyms such as *solamente*).
  - Monosyllabic verb forms take no accent: *fue, vio, dio, ti*.

### 4.3. Subject Handling and Neutral Pronominalization
- **Standard Inclusive Plural**: Throughout Latin America only **ustedes** is used with third-person plural conjugation, banishing the Peninsular *vosotros*.
- **Second-Person Consistency**: Keep the register consistent. If you choose the neutral formal address (*usted*), sustain it: *"Abra el archivo y guarde los cambios"*. If you choose the direct address (*tú*), sustain it: *"Abre el archivo y guarda los cambios"*. Do not alternate both styles within the same document.

## 🛠️ 5. Five-Phase Editorial Review Protocol

```text
[1. Definición del Propósito] -> [2. Poda de Relleno] -> [3. Desambiguación & RAE] -> [4. Filtro Anti-IA] -> [5. Afinación de Cadencia]
```

1. **Phase 1 - Purpose**: Determine the objective the reader must carry out. Remove collateral concepts that distract attention.
2. **Phase 2 - Filler Pruning**: Remove empty introductions, obvious justifications, and redundant epilogues.
3. **Phase 3 - Disambiguation & RAE**: Review antecedents of the pronoun *su*, `¿?` and `¡!` marks, diacritical accents, and false friends.
4. **Phase 4 - Anti-AI Filter**: Track down and eradicate inflated verbs (*apalancar*, *sumergirse*, *empoderar*) and template clichés.
5. **Phase 5 - Cadence Tuning**: Modulate sentence length to achieve agile, precise, and human prose.
