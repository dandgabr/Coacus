# Enterprise Information Systems and IT Governance (Laudon & BPMN)

This skill establishes the strategic alignment between information technology and enterprise business objectives, covering the mapping of operational flows and service governance.

---

## 🏢 1. Business Process Modeling (BPMN 2.0)

- **Events**: Start (thin circle), Intermediate (double circle), End (thick circle).
- **Activities**: User Tasks, Service Tasks (APIs), Subprocesses.
- **Gateways**: Exclusive (XOR - single decision), Parallel (AND - fork/join), Inclusive (OR).
- **Pools & Lanes**: Represent partner organizations and internal execution roles.
