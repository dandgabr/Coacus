# Tandem mass spectra research

1. Record sample and acquisition provenance, instrument conditions, precursor and adduct metadata, collision settings, library identity and relevant quality flags. Preserve raw spectra and any conversion or preprocessing history.
2. Apply compatible preprocessing to query and reference spectra. Document peak filtering, mass-tolerance units and scoring settings; verify that precursor and ion-mode assumptions match the intended comparison.
3. Report candidate matches with their score, coverage, metadata agreement and library scope. A high spectral similarity is evidence for a candidate, not proof of compound identity; use suitable independent confirmation and report ambiguous matches.
4. Preserve identifiers, processing parameters and software environment for reproducibility. Library permissions and data rights are separate from the analysis software's license.
